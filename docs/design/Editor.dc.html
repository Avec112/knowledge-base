This guide walks you through creating, organising and publishing articles in KnowledgeBase. Articles are written in Markdown and grouped into categories in the sidebar.

Create your first article

  1. Select New article in the sidebar.
  2. Give the article a title and pick a category.
  3. Write the content in Markdown and press Save.
mvn spring-boot:run -Dspring-boot.run.profiles=demo

Organise with categories

Categories keep related articles together and can be reordered by dragging them in the sidebar. Articles without a category are listed under Uncategorized.

Tip: Drafts are only visible to administrators until the status is set to Published.
